Sweeter Side by Dachel ("we") runs this website and our café at . This policy says what personal information we collect, why, and what you can ask us to do with it. We follow the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173).

What we collect

  • Online orders: your name, mobile number, and for delivery your address and any notes you add.
  • Custom cake requests: your name, mobile number, the date, the cake details, and any photos you upload to show the design you want.
  • Senior Citizen and PWD discounts: your name and ID number, because the law requires us to record them for the discount.
  • Payments: if you pay with GCash online, PayMongo handles the payment. We do not see or keep your GCash PIN, card or account details. We only get the payment status and reference.
  • Technical data: your IP address and browser details, kept briefly in our server logs to keep the site safe and stop fake orders.

Why we use it

  • To make, deliver and confirm your order or cake, and to call or text you about it.
  • To take payment, give refunds and keep the sales records the law requires.
  • To stop fake orders and keep the site secure.

We do not sell your information. We do not use it to send you advertising unless you ask us to.

Cookies, analytics and ads

The site keeps your cart and order type in your browser so they are still there when you come back. We may use Meta Pixel, Google Analytics, Google Ads and Microsoft Clarity to see how the site is used and to measure our ads. These load only after you tap Accept on the banner, and you can say no. They never receive your name, phone or address. Clarity hides what you type in the checkout and cake forms.

Who we share it with

  • PayMongo, for GCash payments.
  • Our hosting provider (Fly.io, with servers in Singapore), which stores the website and its database.
  • Our staff and riders, only what they need to make and deliver your order.
  • The government, when the law requires it, for example for tax records.

How long we keep it

We keep your information only as long as we need it for your order, our records and the law. Tax and sales records are usually kept for at least five years. When we no longer need something, we delete it. You can ask us to delete your information sooner, and we will, unless the law requires us to keep it.

How we protect it

The site uses HTTPS. Only signed-in staff can see orders, and staff see only what their role needs. Senior Citizen and PWD ID numbers are hidden on most screens. Uploaded cake photos are private and are not shown on the public site.

Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act you can ask to see the information we have about you, correct it, have it deleted or blocked, or object to how we use it. You can also complain to the National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph).
