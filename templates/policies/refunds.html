We make food and cakes fresh for each order, so we cannot take returns. This policy says when you can cancel and when you get your money back.

Cancelling an order

  • Before we start making it: you can cancel and get a full refund of anything you paid.
  • Once we start making it, or it is out for delivery: it can no longer be cancelled or refunded.

To cancel, call or text right away with your order number.

Wrong, missing or damaged items

If an item is wrong, missing or damaged, tell us the same day, with a photo if you can. We will replace it or refund it.

Orders we cancel

If we cancel your order, for example because an item ran out, you get a full refund of anything you paid.

Custom cake deposits

  • If you cancel at least before the pick-up or delivery date, we refund your deposit in full.
  • If you cancel later than that, the deposit is not refunded, because we have already bought the ingredients and set aside the time.

How refunds are paid

  • Paid with GCash online: refunded to the same GCash account through PayMongo. How long it takes to show depends on PayMongo and GCash.
  • Paid in cash: refunded in cash at the café, or by GCash if we agree on it.

Recurring payments

We do not charge recurring payments or subscriptions, so there is nothing to cancel after an order is done.
