Settings

Opening hours, checkout protection and phone alerts. The Open/Closed switch, the delivery fee and order types are on the Orders page.

Opening hours

The same every day, in Philippine time. Online ordering opens and closes with these hours, pick-up times are offered within them, and 30 minutes before closing the menu says "Closing soon". Outside the hours customers can pre-order: pick-up or delivery for a later time, up to the number of days you set (0: today only). To close for a holiday or close early, set the shop to Closed on the Orders page.

Delivery areas

Customers pick their area at checkout and pay that area's fee. An area with no fee yet is not offered. "Other area" is always offered: the order comes in marked "Fee to confirm" and you call the customer with the fee.

    The cart shows "Add ₱X more for free delivery" until the order reaches it. Not for "Other area".

    Cart suggestions

    The cart shows up to three "Goes well with" items, from the categories already in the cart, then the list for any order. Items already in the cart, sold out or off the menu are left out. Items with options open their choices.

    Order confirmation

    After a delivery or pick-up order the customer reads "We'll call you within about N minutes to confirm", with a tap-to-call button and a link to message you on Facebook.

    Share image

    The picture shown when someone shares sweeterside.ph on Facebook, Messenger or X. Best at 1200 × 630 pixels (wide), JPEG or PNG. Without one, the logo is used.

    Fake-order protection

    Blocked numbers are on the Blocked page.

    Order alerts

    A notification on this phone or tablet for every new online order and cake booking, even when the app is closed. It shows the order number, type and total, never the customer's details.

    Checking this device…

    On an iPhone or iPad: alerts work from the Home Screen app.

    1. Open this page in Safari.
    2. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
    3. Open Sweeter Side from the Home Screen, go to Settings and turn alerts on.
